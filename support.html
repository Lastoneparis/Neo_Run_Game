📚 Frequently Asked Questions

How do I play Neo Run?

Neo Run is an endless runner. It's simple to pick up:

  • Tap to jump
  • Double-tap for a double jump
  • Triple-tap for a triple jump
  • Avoid obstacles, collect coins, and survive as long as you can!

The game speeds up the longer you run, so timing your jumps is key to beating your high score.

How do I earn coins?

Coins are collected during gameplay by running through them. You can also:

  • Complete daily missions for bonus coins
  • Build combos for coin multipliers
  • Get daily bonus coins with the VIP Pass
  • Purchase coin packs in the Shop

How do I unlock new skins?

Collect coins during gameplay and use them to purchase skins in the Shop. You can also earn coins by completing daily missions or purchasing coin packs. There are 20+ unique skins to collect!

What are daily missions?

Daily missions refresh every 24 hours and give you specific challenges to complete. Finish missions to earn extra coins and XP! Check the Missions tab to see your current tasks.

How does the level system work?

As you play, you earn XP based on your performance. Collect more coins, build combos, and survive longer to earn more XP. Level up to unlock new content and features!

💡 Tip: The VIP Pass gives you 2x XP to level up faster!

What are power-ups and how do I use them?

Power-ups give you special abilities during gameplay:

  • Speed Boost: Run faster temporarily
  • Shield: Protect yourself from one obstacle
  • Coin Magnet: Auto-collect nearby coins
  • 2x Coins: Double coin collection (VIP perk)

Purchase and activate power-ups from the Shop before starting a run!

How do in-app purchases work?

Neo Run is free to play! In-app purchases are optional and include:

  • Coin packs to unlock skins faster
  • VIP Pass for 2x XP, 2x coins, and exclusive benefits
  • Extra lives to continue your run

All purchases are processed securely through Apple.

What is the VIP Pass?

The VIP Pass ($7.99/month) is an auto-renewing subscription that includes:

  • 2x XP for faster leveling
  • 2x coins on every run
  • Exclusive characters and skins
  • Daily bonus coins

It renews monthly and you can cancel anytime in your iOS Settings.

How do I cancel my VIP Pass subscription?

To cancel your subscription:

  1. Open iOS Settings
  2. Tap your name at the top
  3. Tap Subscriptions
  4. Select Neo Run - VIP Pass
  5. Tap Cancel Subscription

You'll keep VIP benefits until the end of the current billing period.

I made a purchase but didn't receive my items

Try these steps:

  1. Close and restart the app
  2. Check your purchase history in the App Store
  3. Go to Settings → Restore Purchases in the game
  4. Make sure you're connected to the internet

Still having issues? Contact us with your receipt and we'll help! All purchases go through Apple, so you can also contact Apple Support for payment issues.

How do I restore purchases on a new device?

All your purchases are linked to your Apple ID and can be restored for free:

  1. Download Neo Run on your new device
  2. Sign in with the same Apple ID you used for purchases
  3. Open the game and go to Settings
  4. Tap "Restore Purchases"
  5. Your purchases will be restored!

My game progress was lost. Can I recover it?

If you enabled iCloud sync, your progress should automatically restore when you reinstall the app on the same Apple ID account.

To enable iCloud sync:

  • iOS Settings → [Your Name] → iCloud
  • Make sure "Neo Run" is enabled
⚠️ Important: Local data is lost if you delete the app without iCloud sync enabled.

How do leaderboards work?

Leaderboards use Apple Game Center to show your best score compared to other players. Your high score is automatically submitted when you beat your personal record. Game Center is optional — you can play without signing in.

How do I turn off sound, music, or haptics?

Tap the ⚙️ Settings icon on the main menu. From there you can toggle:

  • Sound Effects (on/off)
  • Background Music (on/off)
  • Haptic Feedback (on/off)

The game is lagging or crashing

Try these solutions:

  1. Restart the app — Close completely and reopen
  2. Restart your device — Turn it off and on
  3. Update the app — Check for updates in the App Store
  4. Update iOS — Make sure you're running the latest iOS version
  5. Free up storage — Delete unused apps or photos
  6. Close background apps — Swipe up to close other apps

Still experiencing issues? Contact us with your device model and iOS version.

Can I play offline?

Yes! Neo Run works completely offline. You don't need an internet connection to play. However, you'll need internet to:

  • Make in-app purchases
  • Sync progress with iCloud
  • Submit scores to Game Center leaderboards
  • Update the app

How do I delete my game data?

To reset your progress:

  • In-game: Settings → Reset Progress
  • Complete removal: Delete the Neo Run app from your device
⚠️ Warning: This action cannot be undone!

Is Neo Run safe for children?

Yes! Neo Run is rated 4+ and appropriate for all ages. The game features:

  • ✅ No violence or inappropriate content
  • ✅ No online multiplayer or chat
  • ✅ No collection of personal information
  • ✅ No third-party advertising
  • ✅ Family-friendly gameplay

Parents: You can restrict in-app purchases using iOS Parental Controls.

How do I request a refund?

All purchases are processed by Apple. To request a refund:

  1. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com
  2. Sign in with your Apple ID
  3. Find your Neo Run purchase
  4. Click "Report a Problem"
  5. Select "Request a refund"
  6. Choose a reason and submit

Apple usually responds within 48 hours.
